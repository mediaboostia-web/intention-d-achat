# Tamani — lots 7a and 7b (design pass, countries and currencies)

## Tamani design pass (lot 7a)

The porteur's UX/UI requests after lot 7 ([retours](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-tamani-retours-porteur-3.md), binding; [plan](../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-28-tamani-07a-design.md)); decisions are listed under « Lot 7a » in the roadmap.

- **Night theme** — `nightColors` in [tokens.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/design/tokens.ts) has the day's keys; `contrast.test.ts` checks every `contrastPairs` entry in both palettes and `tokens-sync.test.ts` checks the `:root[data-theme='dark']` block of `globals.css`. `deep` / `deepInk` are the notebook cover (sidebar, `Card tone="ink"`, scrims), dark in both themes: use them, not `bg-ink`, for a surface that must stay dark. At night the stamp is printed plain (`mix-blend-mode: normal`). Only the shop space goes dark: the landing, the sign-in pages and the popup stay in day mode.
- **Shell state on `<html>`** — `data-theme="dark"` and `data-sidebar="collapsed"`, with the `night:` and `collapsed:` variants. The root layout's `<head>` runs `SHELL_PREFS_SCRIPT` before the first paint, only under `/app`; `ShellPrefs` in `app/(app)/app/layout.tsx` (`applyShellPrefs`, a layout effect) sets them after a client navigation and removes them when `/app` is left, even when the gate sends the visitor to sign in before the shell mounts. Storage goes through [prefs.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/prefs.ts) (`readPref` / `writePref` never throw; keys `tamani-theme`, `tamani-sidebar`, `tamani-layout:<tab>`).
- **Shell** — [TopBar.tsx](../../frontend/src/components/app/TopBar.tsx) is the one header at every width (one bell, so the count is polled once): on a phone the shop, day / night, the bell, the profile; from 1024px it sticks and adds `SidebarToggle` (`aria-label="Menu latéral"` + `aria-expanded`) and `GlobalSearch`. `SideNav` folds to 84px: each link keeps `title` and a `collapsed:sr-only` label. `shellWidth(pathname)` ([nav.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/nav.ts)) gives `/app` and the four lists `max-w-[1360px]`, every other page `max-w-3xl`.
- **Search** — `GET /api/shops/[orgId]/search?q=` (`MEMBER`, 2 to `SEARCH_MAX` characters once trimmed, else 400) runs `searchShop` ([search.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/tamani/search.ts)): 5 campaigns, products and clients, each query with `organizationId`, clients through `visibleClientWhere` + `clientSearchWhere` (no digits and no number without `respondentContacts`). `searchOptions` ([search-view.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/search-view.ts)) flattens them for the combobox; `GlobalSearch` waits 250 ms, ignores a stale answer, walks options with the arrows, opens one with Enter, and Ctrl K / ⌘ K focuses it.
- **Home** — `HomeView` renders the to-do, activity, upcoming and money blocks twice (`prefix` `m-` for the phone copy, `d-` for the desktop one): ids stay unique and the phone order is unchanged. From 1024 to 1279px the three actions sit under the title (`DesktopActions`), on the title row from 1280px. Tiles are `VerdictTile` ([VerdictTile.tsx](../../frontend/src/components/ui/VerdictTile.tsx)) with `verdictCalc` / `verdictGauge` ([home-view.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/home-view.ts)); rows below 1024px, tiles from 1024px, the right column from 1280px.
- **Cards or list** — `CampaignCard` and `ProductCard` take `layout: 'card' | 'list'` (card by default: `BANNER` photo, 16:7, lazy; list: 44px `THUMB`); `LayoutSwitch` and `useListLayout(tab)` keep the choice per tab; `listClass(layout)` gives one card per row on a phone, 240px cards from 640px.
- **Popup** — the words are in [popup-copy.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/popup-copy.ts) (`stockLine`, `thanksCopy`, `poweredByHref`); `Picto` ([Picto.tsx](../../frontend/src/components/ui/Picto.tsx)) draws the two-tone stall, cartons and calendar. The `thanks` step renders [thanks-dialog.tsx](../../frontend/src/app/(public)/c/[code]/thanks-dialog.tsx) (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, focus on its title, Tab kept inside) while the sheet behind is `inert`. `changeMind` in `popup-machine.ts` turns a finished no into a yes and goes to the buyer question; it posts nothing (the contact step resends every answer), so a tap then a closed page leaves the « non » as it was. Under 640px of height the dialog is compact. `PopupSheet` takes `homeUrl` (`APP_URL`) for « Propulsé par Tamani », opened in a new tab.
- **Landing on a phone** — below 1000px `.lp-nav__login` and `.lp-nav__cta` are hidden and the bar has no band until it comes back on the way up; the menu is the `.lp-drawer` (`#lp-menu`, `role="dialog"`, `inert` while closed) from the right, links staggered by `--i`, ending on « Commencer gratuitement » then « Se connecter ». Escape, the backdrop or the close button shut it; it also closes when the window grows past 1000px.

## Tamani countries and currencies (lot 7b)

All of French-speaking Africa, one currency per shop ([plan](../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-29-tamani-07b-pays-monnaies.md)); the porteur's decisions are listed under « Lot 7b » in the roadmap.

- **Countries** — `SHOP_COUNTRIES` ([countries.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/countries.ts)), in the select's order: the 8 UEMOA countries (XOF), the 6 CEMAC ones (XAF), Guinea (GNF) and DR Congo (CDF), each with its dial code and `currency`. The API refuses any other country (`isShopCountry` in `ShopCreate` / `ShopPatch`).
- **Currency** — `Organization.currency` follows the country (`currencyForCountry`) at creation and at a country change. Migration `9b_currency_by_country` fixed the shops made before (all were XOF). Amounts stay integers in whole units, as the money is counted at the market. ISO 4217 gives CDF two minor units (XOF, XAF and GNF none): lot 8 decides how a CDF amount is sent to SasPay, whose amounts are decimal strings.
- **Display** — [currency.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/currency.ts): `formatMoney(amount, currency)` and `currencyLabel(currency)` write the local name (« FCFA » for XOF and XAF, « FG », « FC »); the ISO code never reaches a screen (it is for SasPay). Every amount goes through them: `formatPriceLine(p, currency)`, `previewMessage(template, product, availability, now, currency, link?)`, `orderPriceText(p, currency)`, `orderWhatsappMessage({ …, currency })`, `productFormToBody(v, currency)` (its hint names the currency).
- **Where the currency comes from** — views and kit components that show money take a required `currency` prop (`MoneyCard`, `ProductCard`, `ProductForm`, `OrderForm`, `OrderDetailView`, `OrderListView`, `OrderLines`, `ClientDetailView`, `CampaignResults` and `CampaignOrdersView` with their tabs, `CampaignWizard`, `CampaignEditForm`); screens pass `useShop().shop.currency`, so static tests stay free of context and `tsc` finds a forgotten caller. The server reads it with `shopCurrency(db, orgId)` ([shop.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/tamani/shop.ts)) for `toCampaign(c, now, currency)`; the popup and its link preview read `PublicCampaign.shop.currency`.
- **Tripwire** — [no-fcfa-literal.test.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/observability/no-fcfa-literal.test.ts) fails on the word « FCFA » on any line that is not a comment, and on `formatPrice(x, label)` with its own label, outside `lib/utils.ts`, `currency.ts`, the landing, the plans, the payments and the styleguide: Tamani's own prices stay in FCFA until lot 8.
- **Typed prices** — `parseAmount` ([fcfa-input.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/fcfa-input.ts)) drops a trailing FCFA, CFA, F, FG, GNF, FC or CDF: « 65 000 FG » is 65000.
- **Country lock** — the country, and so the currency, changes only while the shop has no product, archived ones included (`shopHasProducts`): prices typed in FCFA must never read as Guinean francs. `PATCH /api/shops/[orgId]` runs in a transaction: a body with a country takes `advisoryXactLock(tx, shopCountryLock(orgId))` (`shop-country:<orgId>`), then reads the current country, refuses a move once a product exists (`409 SHOP_COUNTRY_LOCKED`, before the number is checked against the new country) and writes `currency` with any country sent. Every product creation takes the same lock, so a first product racing a country change cannot slip under it, and two tabs cannot leave a country with another's currency. The same country sent again is no change. `toShop(org, sub, now, countryLocked)` sends `countryLocked`. Réglages sends the country only when it changes (`shopSettingsBody` in [shop-settings.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/shop-settings.ts)), greys the select out with a lock and says why (a disabled `Select` drops its ink frame and arrow), and on a 409 reloads the shop and remounts the form; the new-product page reloads the shop after the first product.
- **Numbers** — every shop country is a libphonenumber region. `normalizePhone` turns an old 8-digit Benin number, typed locally or after `+229`, into the 10-digit one with « 01 » in front (Benin moved to 10 digits in November 2024). Old numbers never started with 0, so « 01 97 12 34 » is a new number cut short and is refused.
- **Not here** — SasPay's coverage per country and the 70 % margin per currency are checked in lot 8. The export has no money column.
