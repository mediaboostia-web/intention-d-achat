# Tamani — lot 11 (support-admin)

## Tamani support-admin (lot 11)

The back-office the porteur asked for on 2026-10-30 ([plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-tamani-11-support-admin.md), definitions validated on 2026-11-04); decisions are listed under « Lot 11 » in the roadmap.

- **Address** — `/support-admin` (`ADMIN_HOME` in [admin-nav.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/admin-nav.ts)); the kit's `/admin` redirects there. [proxy.ts](../../frontend/src/proxy.ts) protects `/support-admin` wherever `AUTH_PROTECTED_PREFIXES` lists `/admin`. `robots.ts` does not name it (that would publish the path). Pages are server components: `adminOnly()` ([guard.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/tamani/admin/guard.ts)) runs in the layout **and** in each page (Next renders them side by side): no session → `/login?next=%2Fsupport-admin`, signed in without `ADMIN` → `notFound()`, so nobody learns the back-office exists. `AdminShell` / `AdminNav` ([components/admin/](../../frontend/src/components/admin/)) draw the cover header and the four sections; tables are `AdminTable` (scrolls sideways inside its frame).
- **Reads and writes** — every page reads with `requireAdmin('ADMIN')`; money, passes and accounts are written by a `SUPERADMIN` only (`requireSuperadmin()`), and an `ADMIN` does not even see those forms. Each write route: `withRequestContext` → `verifyCsrf` → role → `enforceAdminRateLimit` → zod → `prisma.$transaction` with `logAdminAction(tx, …)` → JSON (never 204). Client islands (`MarketingSpendForm`, `GrantPassForm`, `EndPassButton`, `CreateUserForm`, `UserActions`) call `api()` then `router.refresh()`.
- **Dashboard** (`/support-admin?mois=AAAA-MM`, UTC months, never past the current one) — `loadAdminDashboard(db, month, now)` ([dashboard.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/tamani/admin/dashboard.ts)) over pure rules in [metrics.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/tamani/admin/metrics.ts); words in [admin-view.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/admin-view.ts) (each figure writes its calculation under it). Amounts are XOF and XAF summed as FCFA (`CFA_FRANCS`); a manual grant is stored in XOF.
  - **Encaissé**: activated `Order`s of the month (`activatedAt` set, amount > 0), by pass and by provider (`saspay` / `manual`), and the list of payments.
  - **Coverage**: a shop's activated pass orders replayed in order; an order before the previous one's end plus `GRACE_DAYS` (3) extends the coverage, a later one opens a new one.
  - **Churn**: shops covered at the month's first instant whose coverage ends in the month with no purchase after it that month ÷ shops covered at the start; `null` with none.
  - **CAC**: the month's typed marketing spend ÷ shops whose first paid order falls in the month; `null` without spend or new payer. The spend is `MarketingSpend { month @id, amount, updatedById }` (migration `9h_marketing_spend`), typed by a super-admin through `PUT /api/admin/marketing-spend` `{ month, amount }` (audit `marketing.spend_set`, `{ from, to }`).
  - **MRR (now)**: each running paid pass at `price × 30 ÷ days`; Illimité from its last manual grant (0 without an amount); trial, grace and « Sans pass » count 0.
  - **Shops**: active (effective plan not `FREE`, trial and grace included) out of all, by plan, by `businessType` and by `salesChannel` (« Non renseigné » for older shops). **Campaigns**: running, drafts, finished, archived (`countCampaigns` in [counts.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/tamani/admin/counts.ts)). Accounts: total and suspended.
- **Shops** — `/support-admin/boutiques?q=&forfait=&page=` (`listAdminShops`: name or owner email, filter on the effective plan in memory — at most 5 000 read, 50 per page) and `/support-admin/boutiques/[id]` (`getAdminShop`: owner, pass usage, campaigns, the 20 latest purchases).
- **Grant a pass by hand** — `POST /api/admin/shops/[orgId]/pass` `{ plan: PASS_IDS | 'UNLIMITED', amount 0..1e8, days?, responses? }` (Illimité needs `days` and `responses`) → `201 { orderId, endsAt }`, `404 SHOP_NOT_FOUND`. [grant.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/tamani/admin/grant.ts): a `provider: 'manual'` `Order` (`PAID`, `expiresAt` now, metadata `{ days, responses }`), then the purchase path itself — `billingLock` then `quotaLock`, `applyGrant` (the carry-over of `applyPurchase`, which now delegates to it), `unlockPending`, a `passActivated` bell. Audit `shop.pass_grant` with the terms, amount and order id. `DELETE` ends the pass now (plan `FREE`, dates and answers cleared) → `{ ended: true }`, audit `shop.pass_end` `{ from }`.
- **Accounts** — `/support-admin/utilisateurs?q=&page=` and `/support-admin/utilisateurs/[id]` ([users.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/tamani/admin/users.ts), words in [admin-users.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/admin-users.ts)). `POST /api/admin/users` `{ email, name }` (`SUPERADMIN`) creates a verified account with a 12-character temporary password (`temporaryPassword`, no 0/O/1/l/I) → `201 { user, temporaryPassword }` with `cache-control: no-store`, `409 EMAIL_TAKEN`; the password shows once in `CreateUserForm` and is never logged or audited (audit `user.create` `{ role }`). `PATCH /api/admin/users/[id]` `{ name }` (`ADMIN`, audit `user.rename` without the name). Role and suspension go through the kit's `…/role` (super-admin) and `…/status` routes (restore, and suspending a super-admin, are a super-admin's call); one's own account is never offered « Suspendre ».
- **Journal** — `/support-admin/journal`: the 100 newest `AdminAction` rows (`loadJournal`, [journal.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/server/tamani/admin/journal.ts)) with the actor's email, the target named and linked (shop, account, month), one line on what changed, and the time in Cotonou ([admin-journal.ts](../../frontend/src/lib/tamani/admin-journal.ts): `actionLabel`, `actionDetail` — metadata read field by field — `targetHref`, `journalWhen`). An unknown action keeps its code.
- **Tests** — route tests use [admin-contexts.ts](../../frontend/src/test-utils/admin-contexts.ts) (`ADMIN_CTX`, `SUPERADMIN_CTX`, `SUPERADMIN_REQUIRED()`, `adminRequest`); views are static HTML; loaders use `prismaMock`.
